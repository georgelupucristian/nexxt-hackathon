KIP: HOW EVERYTHING WORKS
Demo workflow vs real business workflow, for the pitch and the presentation
Last updated: 29 Oct 2026

=====================================================================
0. ONE-LINE SUMMARY
=====================================================================
Kip is a savings app for students. You pick a goal, put aside a small
amount every day, keep a streak, save with friends and unlock clubs
with partner discounts. Kip never holds the money: in the real product
it sits in the student's own savings account at a licensed Romanian
bank: whichever bank they already use, with Libra Internet Bank and
Raiffeisen Bank as recommended partners. Kip is the app on top.

Live: https://listo-solutions.ro (on a laptop it shows inside a phone frame)
Language: English. Currency: lei (RON).

2. THE STUDENT'S JOURNEY (WHAT THE APP DOES TODAY)
=====================================================================
2.1 Welcome
- Full-screen video of Kip, "Saving on a student budget? Totally doable."
- Buttons: Get started / I already have an account / Try with demo data.

2.2 Onboarding (one question per screen, Kip asks in a speech bubble)
1. Age range
2. Why you're saving (multi-select)
3. How much per day (5 / 10 / 15 / 20 lei), with the monthly total
4. What that becomes in 3 months
5. How much you already have saved (counts toward the goal from day 1;
   no money moves; starts at 0)
6. Phone number, then the 6-digit code
7. Name
8. Where your money comes from (family, part-time job, scholarship...)
9. Notifications
10. Face ID / fingerprint to lock the app on this phone (optional)
11. Contacts: "So you can find friends who already save" (optional)
    and "Friends can find me by my number" (can be turned off later)
12. Your plan, then home
NO CARD IS ASKED FOR DURING SIGN-UP.

2.3 Home
- Streak card: days in a row, this week as 7 circles (fire = saved day,
  snowflake = frozen day).
- Goals card: saved this month, lei per day to finish on time, a ring
  with Kip, the list of goals (personal and shared).
- "Save money" button.
- Recent activity (a manual entry can be undone).
- Clubs row ("Clubs: 2 of 3 slots").
- "Sleep on it": park something you want to buy for 48 hours.
- Kip's button at the bottom: Home / Goals / Activity / Profile.
- Header: Friends (people icon), notifications, "?" replays the tour.
- A first-run tour (8 stops) explains each part with small animations.

2.4 Goals
- New goal wizard: just me / with friends, what for, how much,
  by when, then a plan Kip works out.
- Goal page: progress, Deposit / Withdraw, plan, icon, pause,
  archive, delete.
- Plan: every week or every month, which day, how much each time.

2.5 Putting money in (demo)
- "Save money" opens "Move money into your savings":
  "It goes from your card into your Kip savings, not to us. Take it out
  anytime." plus "Where's my money?".
- The card is asked for ONLY here, the first time the student chooses
  to move money. "Your card is only used to move money into your
  savings."
- Stripe Checkout: "Transfer to your Kip savings", "Kip takes no fee".
  The first collection happens on the chosen day.
  * Chosen day is today: the money moves today.
  * Chosen day is later: Stripe only SAVES THE CARD ("Kip saves your
    card to move money into your own savings. Nothing is charged today.
    Your first X lei goes in on <date>."). The server then starts the
    weekly or monthly transfer exactly on that day.
  * Why: an earlier version delayed the first charge with a Stripe
    "trial", and Stripe always labels a trial "N days free", which made
    it look like a free trial of a product. Saving the card instead
    removes any "free"/"trial" wording.
- The name shown at the top of Stripe's page is the Stripe account's
  public business name; it must be set to "Kip" in the Stripe dashboard
  (Settings > Business > Public details).
- Stripe confirms through a webhook; the money appears in the app by
  itself, then Kip celebrates.
- Extra deposits: now (saved card) or with the next collection.
- Withdraw: "Move money back to your card" (simulated in the demo).

3. STREAKS AND FREEZES
=====================================================================
- ONE rule for the whole app (computed on the server): a day counts
  only if that day's deposits reach the student's daily amount
  (min 1 leu). Depositing 10x the amount is still one day. Withdrawals
  don't touch the streak. There is NO "tap to check in" button.
- 1-day Freeze: everyone has at most 1. If you miss a day, it's used
  automatically and the streak survives (a frozen day doesn't grow
  the streak). It recharges 7 days after it was used.
- Saving a friend: if a friend missed a day and has no freeze, they are
  "In danger" for 24h and can tap "Ask for a freeze". You can give them
  YOUR 1-day Freeze ("If you give them your freeze, you'll have no
  protection until <date>"). It only works if the friend saves within
  24h; otherwise it comes back to you. Max 2 rescues per person per 30
  days.
- 1-week Freeze (earned every 45 days in a row): designed; demo only.

=====================================================================
4. SOCIAL: FRIENDS, SHARED GOALS, CLUBS
=====================================================================
4.1 Friends (real, on the live database)
- Found by phone number. Contact matching sends only numbers, never
  names, and never stores the numbers of people without Kip.
- "Add" sends a request; you're friends only after they accept.
  No limit on friends; max 50 requests per day (anti-spam).
- Friends see ONLY: name, avatar, streak, saved today yes/no, freeze
  status. NEVER amounts.
- Cheers: "Well done!", "You've got this!", "I'm here if you need me"
  (max 3 a day per friend).
- Invite link: when a friend joins with your link, your 1-day Freeze
  recharges right away. (We dropped "2 bonus streak days": a streak
  should only grow from real saving.)
- Remove and block.

4.2 Shared goals (real, on the live database)
- Created from "New goal" > "With friends": pick up to 4 friends.
- Friends get an invite (Join / No thanks) and their share (equal by
  default, editable).
- A contribution is EXTRA money, separate from personal savings: a new
  deposit made for the shared goal.
- Money never moves between people. Each person's contributions stay
  theirs; each can withdraw only their own. Leaving gives yours back.
- Everyone sees the total % and each member's % of their own share,
  never other people's amounts. Celebration at 25/50/75/100%.
- More friends can be invited later (up to 5 people).
- Paying one organiser at 100% (e.g. booking the trip) is out of scope;
  it needs real person-to-person payments.
- Where it shows: in the same goals list as personal goals (home and
  Goals), marked with the members' avatars and "With Ana, Mihai". No
  separate tab. Invites arrive in Friends > Requests (Join / No thanks).
- Colonies (2 to 5 friends, max 3 per person) are separate: a group that
  keeps a shared streak. A shared goal does not need a colony.

4.3 Clubs (real, on the live database)
- 6 clubs made by our team: Travel, Erasmus, Tech, First apartment,
  My car, Emergency fund. Students can't create clubs.
- Slots grow ONLY with the current streak:
  at sign-up 1 / 14 days 2 / 30 days 3 / 60 days 4 / 90 days 5 /
  150 days 6.
- Unlock a slot: Kip celebrates, "You unlocked a new club! Pick where
  you go."
- Streak breaks (missed day, no freeze): the streak starts again from
  day 1. Clubs above what the new streak allows are PAUSED for 30 days,
  with everything kept. Save 14 days in a row within those 30 days and
  you keep them all ("You're back!"). Otherwise they move to "Clubs you
  can rejoin", still showing their promotions and "N more days to
  rejoin". Blame-free wording, never "you lost".
- Monthly promotions from several companies per club (demo: fictional
  names). New ones every 1st of the month, marked "New". Members claim
  a code once. Non-members see every promotion in full, with "3 more
  streak days to join" instead of Claim.
- How many clubs you're in is private.

=====================================================================
5. THE MONEY MECHANICS IN THE REAL PRODUCT
=====================================================================
5.1 Opening the savings account (no manual work for us)
1. In Kip: "Open your savings account" deep-links into Libra's online
   onboarding.
2. Libra does the KYC itself: ID card, selfie, check against the
   population register. Under 5 minutes, done by the student.
3. Back in Kip, the student connects the account (PSD2 consent). Kip
   reads the balance (AIS) and can start transfers the student has
   approved (PIS / standing order).
4. Phase 2, with a contract: one sub-account per goal, created
   automatically through Libra's API.

5.2 The weekly "reserve and sweep" (the holding mechanic)
Goal: no daily transfer fees, no nagging, and the student never owes Kip
anything.
- Every day: the daily amount is RESERVED on the student's own current
  account (blocked funds or a "notice pot" in the student's name at the
  bank). The money stays the student's, at the bank, but is set aside.
  A day lights the streak when its reserve succeeds.
- Once a week (sweep day, e.g. Sunday): all reserved money for the week
  moves in ONE transfer into the student's savings account. One
  transfer a week means low or no fees (batching).
- Not enough money on a day (e.g. planned 40 lei this week, only 30
  on the account): Kip does NOT send "pay Kip" reminders again and again.
    * The reserve is tried once that day. If it fails, that day isn't
      lit, one quiet note appears in the app ("Not enough today, we'll
      try again tomorrow"), and nothing carries over as a debt.
    * Days that were reserved still sweep normally (30 lei moves, not 0).
    * The student can lower the amount or skip a week in one tap.
    * The freeze still protects the streak for one missed day.
- Streak breaks (missed day, no freeze): Kip does NOT start asking for
  money for each day. It sweeps right away everything reserved so far
  in that streak into the savings account, then a new streak starts
  clean. Missed days are never collected later.
- Hold length: the reserve lasts until the weekly sweep (up to 7 days).
  The exact maximum depends on what Libra allows (possibly 4 to 5
  days). If it's shorter, the sweep simply runs more often (e.g. twice
  a week).
- The demo doesn't do this yet. It collects weekly or monthly by card
  through Stripe, and Stripe retries a failed charge automatically on
  later days ("Change card" is offered). In a demo version, Stripe card
  authorisations (holds lasting about 7 days) could stand in for the
  daily reserve, captured once a week. Not built.

5.3 Withdrawals
- Real: the bank moves money from savings back to the current account.
  Optional 48h "cool-off" friction in the app before big withdrawals.
- Demo: recorded, no payout.

5.4 Parents (designed, partly built in the backend)
- A parent can match the student's saving (e.g. 1:1 up to 150 lei a
  month). Real: the parent sends a RoPay or bank transfer straight to the
  student's IBAN; Kip records the pledge and checks it through AIS.

=====================================================================
6. BUSINESS MODEL
=====================================================================
Kip never takes money from the student's savings and charges no fee on
deposits. Revenue:
1. Club promotions: partner companies (travel, tech, driving schools,
   furniture...) pay an affiliate commission per claimed code or
   purchase. Clubs are segmented by goal, so offers are relevant.
2. Introducer fee from the bank per savings account opened through Kip.
   Students are a young segment banks want, and we bring them already
   saving.
3. Later: Kip Plus, a small subscription (more club slots, extra
   freezes, deeper insights).

7. WHICH BANK: ANY BANK, TWO RECOMMENDED PARTNERS
=====================================================================
The student keeps the bank they already use. Kip is not tied to one
bank:
- ANY Romanian bank with open banking (PSD2): Banca Transilvania, BCR,
  ING, Raiffeisen, CEC, UniCredit, Libra, Revolut and others. Kip reads
  the savings balance (AIS) and the money moves through a standing
  order the student sets once, or a transfer they approve (PIS). Works
  everywhere, no deal needed.
- RECOMMENDED PARTNERS: Libra Internet Bank and Raiffeisen Bank. With a
  partner deal we get the full experience: the daily reserve and the
  automatic weekly sweep (section 5.2), one savings sub-account per
  goal, and opening the account from inside Kip in minutes. Plain open
  banking can't place holds or create sub-accounts, so with other banks
  saving is a simple weekly transfer.
- In the app: "Which bank do you use?" Pick any. Kip suggests Libra or
  Raiffeisen for the full Kip experience, never forces it.

Why Libra Internet Bank as the first partner:
- Already works with fintechs on RON: gives RON IBANs to Wise, Paysera,
  ZEN, iCard, TransferGo, Ebury; has a dedicated fintech desk
  (librabank.ro/fintech).
- Has PSD2 APIs for reading balances and starting transfers.
- Fast online onboarding (under 5 minutes, ID checked in real time),
  which suits students.
- Honest status: nothing signed, no contact yet. Libra's standard
  fintech product is built for licensed firms, so Kip needs a custom
  deal: the pitch proposes a pilot with 100 students.
- Backup: Raiffeisen Bank Romania. Under-25 zero fees, 8-minute digital
  onboarding, and it presents this hackathon (mentors on site), so it is
  also worth naming in the pitch. It has no fintech partnership
  programme yet.

8. LEGAL AND REGULATORY (SHORT)
=====================================================================
- Holding client money would make Kip a credit institution, payment
  institution (Law 209/2019, PSD2) or e-money institution
  (Law 210/2019): a BNR licence and EUR 125k to 350k+ capital. Kip avoids
  this by NEVER holding money: introducer and software provider; in
  phase 2, an outsourced tech provider to the bank under the banking
  outsourcing rules.
- Payment initiation / account information needs a licensed AISP/PISP
  or the bank's own contract.
- KYC/AML: done by the bank.
- GDPR: friends see no amounts; contact matching stores no numbers of
  non-users; amounts and club counts stay private.
- Users must be 18+ (minors excluded in this version).

9. TECHNOLOGY (FOR THE TECH SLIDE)
=====================================================================
- App: React + TypeScript PWA (installable), phone-first, on Vercel.
- Backend: Supabase (Postgres with row-level security on every table,
  server-side functions, scheduled jobs).
- Payments: Stripe sandbox (Checkout, recurring collections, webhooks
  that confirm money exactly once, test clocks).
- Server rules: friends, freezes, streaks, clubs and money limits are
  checked on the server, not just in the app.
- Tests: about 100 app tests and 85+ backend tests, 170+ database
  security tests (one user can't see or change another's data; one
  Stripe event can never be counted twice), Playwright end-to-end tests
  on phone sizes, an accessibility audit, CI on GitHub Actions.
- Docs in the repo: architecture, assumptions and limits, testing,
  test accounts, clubs and social schemas.

12. HONEST LIMITATIONS
=====================================================================
- No bank partner signed yet (Libra and Raiffeisen are targets; any PSD2 bank works for basic saving); payments are Stripe sandbox (test money).
- In the demo the money sits in Kip's Stripe test balance. That's only
  OK because it's test money; the real product uses the student's own
  bank account.
- The daily reserve + weekly sweep is designed, not built.
- SMS codes are fixed (123456) in the sandbox.
- Promotions use fictional companies.
- The 1-week Freeze, colony challenges, leaderboards and club sessions
  are designed but not live.
- Shared goals: paying in by real card is wired but only tested through
  the test-account path; after leaving a shared goal, the money returned
  can't yet be withdrawn from the app (the server supports it).
- "Which bank do you use?" is described here, not built in the app.
